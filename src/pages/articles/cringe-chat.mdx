AI is everywhere these days, so it’s not exciting anymore. I recently realized that what feels exciting to us is not some magic generated by a computer, but rather things that are imperfect and human.

This is why I created the library [human-replay](https://github.com/einenlum/human-replay) which captures how people type and then replays it in the same way, with all the typos, corrections, and pauses. It brings back a sense of humanness that we now miss in our daily interactions.

Since I now had the tools to capture and replay human typing, I started wondering what fun application I could build with it. And I thought: wouldn't it be cringe af if, when chatting with someone online, you could see exactly how they typed their messages?

Here comes [Cringe Chat](https://cringe-chat.einenlum.com).

<NonSourcedImage src="https://res.cloudinary.com/dybam16gk/image/upload/v1769443830/blog_images/cringe-chat_qq7gxp.png" alt="Cringe Chat logo" />

To prevent abuse and bad behavior (this is only an experiment), you need to know the username of the person you want to chat with. You can easily test it in two different browser windows. You can also use the "Connect to self" button to see how you type your own messages.

<NonSourcedImage src="https://res.cloudinary.com/dybam16gk/image/upload/v1769444925/blog_images/cringe-demo_ar2zqz.gif" alt="Cringe Chat demo" />

The project was built with [FastAPI](https://fastapi.tiangolo.com/) and uses WebSockets and [human-replay](https://github.com/einenlum/human-replay) under the hood.
